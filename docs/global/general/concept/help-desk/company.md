# Company Help Desk — Concept

## Required reads

- @~/.teleclaude/docs/general/concept/help-desk/overview.md

## What

A company help desk sits in the company's home and serves that company's
external customers. They arrive through the company desk's entry link.

The agent answers from what the company has made public: the company's own
public documentation and the public documentation of every project the company
owns, together with the public help-desk documentation. Nothing internal to the
company or its projects is available to it on a customer's behalf, and it never
speaks for another company.

An escalation from this desk reaches everyone in its scope: the company's
members and admins, and the platform's admins.

## Why

A customer of a company asks about the company and its products as one thing.
One desk that reads the company's whole public surface answers them without
the customer needing to know how the company divides its work into projects.
