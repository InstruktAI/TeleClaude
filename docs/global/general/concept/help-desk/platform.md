# Platform Help Desk — Concept

## Required reads

- @~/.teleclaude/docs/general/concept/help-desk/overview.md

## What

The platform help desk sits in the TeleClaude home and serves internal users
who have no personal assistant: registered people whose role is contributor or
newcomer. They reach it by messaging the platform without an entry
identifier; their role routes them here. Members and admins have their own
assistants and are not routed here, and a customer never is: the platform desk
has no entry identifier.

The agent answers from the documentation its visitor may read. That depth is
the visitor's own — decided by their identity and each snippet's visibility —
so two visitors can receive different answers to the same question.

An escalation from this desk reaches the platform's admins.

## Why

Internal users below member still need a place to ask how the platform and its
work are organised. Reading at the visitor's own depth lets one desk serve them
all without showing anyone more than their role already reaches.
