# Calendar Briefing — Procedure

## Required reads

@~/.teleclaude/docs/personal/policy/assistant-conduct.md
@~/.teleclaude/docs/personal/spec/person-knowledge.md
@~/.teleclaude/docs/personal/spec/tools/gog-cli.md

## Goal

Give the person the shape of their day or week — commitments, collisions, and
the decisions only they can make.

## Preconditions

- The person is resolved and their workspace is the working directory.
- Their own knowledge has been discovered and read.
- The conduct policy and the tool contract are in force.

## Steps

1. **Read the window** across the calendars that belong to them.
2. **Name what collides** — overlapping commitments, and back-to-back items in
   different places where travel makes the second impossible.
3. **Surface what needs them**: invitations awaiting a response, and anything
   their standing instructions mark as theirs to decide.
4. **Propose changes rather than making them.** Creating, moving, cancelling and
   responding to invitations are outward-facing: they reach other people. They
   happen only under an explicit ask or a standing instruction that covers them,
   and are reported afterwards like any mandated write.
5. **Report once**, in the order the day runs.

## Outputs

- A briefing of the window with collisions and decisions named.
- Any calendar change made under mandate, reported.

## Recovery

- **A calendar cannot be read.** Say which one, and brief on the rest rather
  than failing silently.
- **A collision has no obvious resolution.** Present it; the trade-off between
  two commitments is the person's.
